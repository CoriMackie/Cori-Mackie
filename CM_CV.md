# Cori Mackie

Born and raised in Hawai'i, Cori Mackie has spent her career connecting
people, ideas, and opportunities. After living and working in New York,
she returned home and built a career spanning fashion and retail, the
nonprofit world, and philanthropy.

Her experience includes senior philanthropic work at the Honolulu Museum
of Art, where she developed donor engagement strategies, cultivated
individual, corporate, and foundation relationships, and created
opportunities to strengthen long-term engagement. Earlier in her career,
she led retail and merchandising strategy, including regional leadership
for Prada Hawai'i and her own retail consulting business.

Cori earned her B.A. in English from the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa
and is currently pursuing her Executive MBA at the Shidler College of
Business.

## Areas of Interest

-   Philanthropy and donor engagement
-   Relationship building
-   Business strategy
-   Community engagement
-   Retail and brand strategy
-   Data-informed decision making
